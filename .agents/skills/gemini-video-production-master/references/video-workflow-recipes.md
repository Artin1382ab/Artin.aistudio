# Production playbooks

## Branded 30-second vertical product advertisement
1. Brief: audience, product truth, CTA, brand constraints, desired language, 9:16 or updated platform specification.
2. Media ingest: list each source file with duration, codec, rotation, audio tracks, timecode.
3. Shot selects: identify hero product, demonstration, real feature/benefit, social proof if documented, CTA.
4. Paper edit: 0–3s hook; 3–12s product story; 12–25s proof/detail; 25–30s CTA, then adjust for creative needs.
5. Rough cut: preserve footage, review sound-off on mobile, refine pace.
6. Captions/voice: corrected Persian/English SRT and safe typography, properly licensed audio.
7. Brand safety: check logo and package identity; no hallucinated labels.
8. Export: preview, format variants, metadata QA, evidence and deliverable manifest.

## 1-hour interview to 5-minute film
Inventory > speech transcription > diarization > subject index > paper edit > section timecodes > silence candidates > B-roll cover > captions > mix > QC.
Keep source timecodes for every substantive claim, verify exact quotations, preserve conversational meaning.

## Multicam product demonstration
Inspect sample clocks/timecode > sync with audio clap fallback > isolate correct product SKU > color match > group angles > cut to product detail at the right moment > report mismatched takes.

## Motion graphics / CGI video
Storyboard > text and trademark reference > frame-based timeline in Remotion > Blender 3D assets if actually available > local preview > color/audio QC > signed-off export.

## Offline planning fallback
If the user only supplied an asset list and this is a Gemini Tasks Skill without tools, provide an edit-decision table, timecoded draft, ready-to-run FFmpeg/Remotion steps, and explicit environment prerequisites; never claim video was rendered.
