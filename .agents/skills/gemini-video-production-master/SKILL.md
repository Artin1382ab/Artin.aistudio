## End-to-end procedure
1. **Brief / preflight:** Record deliverable formats, brand rules, duration, audience, media sources, language and rights. If the user supplied their brief, use it; never ask repetitive questions.
2. **Source ingest / visual analysis:** If files are accessible, inspect clips for codec, timecode, audio and shot boundaries. Otherwise identify the missing operational inputs clearly.
3. **Creative and technical planning:** Give a timecoded storyboard/shot list, music/VO map, title sequence, safe areas and quality criteria.
4. **Rough cut / motion:** Use actual connected tools for footage editing; Remotion for typography and graphics; Blender/ComfyUI/ML models only when installed and authorized.
5. **Audio, captions and visual review:** Transcribe, fix Persian RTL captions, check transitions, preserve exact logo and SKU labels, ensure sound sync and sound-off comprehensibility.
6. **Preview and approval:** Produce an affordable proxy when the runtime is available; otherwise supply detailed instructions and say no preview was rendered.
7. **Export and QA:** Confirm codec, dimensions, time duration, rights, platform specs, subtitles and visible brand identity. Save final paths and checksums if actually generated.
